FAQ

Common Questions

We've heard it all. Here are answers to the things people ask us most.

Most screen and battery replacements are done in 1–2 hours. More complex repairs like charging ports and water damage treatment take 3–6 hours. Motherboard-level repairs may take 1–2 days. We'll give you a realistic time estimate when you book.
Yes — every repair comes with a 90-day warranty. If the exact same fault recurs within 90 days due to our work or the parts we used, we repair it free of charge. This doesn't cover new physical damage, water damage, or different faults.
Water damage recovery depends on how quickly you bring it in and how much liquid got in. We use ultrasonic cleaning on circuit boards and have a good success rate when devices are brought in within 24–48 hours. Avoid charging or turning it on if it got wet — power can cause more damage.
Absolutely. We do not access your personal data, apps, photos or messages. Our technicians only work on the hardware component that needs repair. For screen and battery replacements, your data is fully intact. For major board repairs, we recommend backing up your data first as a precaution — and we'll remind you when you book.
We accept M-Pesa (our preferred method), cash, and major bank cards. Payment is collected after your device is repaired and ready for collection — not upfront. For parts that need to be ordered, a small deposit may be required.
Yes — we offer device collection and drop-off via trusted couriers within Nairobi. A pickup/delivery fee applies based on your location. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange. We currently serve Westlands, CBD, Kilimani, Karen, Kasarani, and surrounding areas.
If after diagnosis we determine the repair isn't feasible or the cost exceeds the device's value, we'll tell you honestly. No charge for the diagnosis in that case. We'll also advise on whether a replacement or trade-in makes more sense.
